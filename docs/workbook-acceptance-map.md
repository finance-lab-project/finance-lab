# Workbook acceptance map

Issue #78 · December 5 release · M7 plan item 32 · sprint start 2026-11-28 (Moscow).

The 16 areas and formula-cell counts come from the approved anonymized inspection. The [Budget scope](product-scope.md) and current application determine coverage. Examples use synthetic inputs. Formulas for proposed screens are acceptance examples. Exact workbook behavior outside the approved evidence requires private comparison, including the cases called out below.

| Portfolio risk map · covered weights and look-through | Use dated holding values and exposure fractions. Divide each value by total covered value. Sum value × fraction by exposure and divide by covered value for exposure weight. Keep planning income separate from actual income. | March fixture income 50,000, spending 20,000 and contributions 10,000. Food plan 24,000 − actual 6,000 = 18,000 remaining. | `/months/{month}` |
| Annual spending/income · carry | Sum saved plan − actual for recorded earlier completed months, including across years. | RUB entering selected month | Skip periods without recorded plans. | FIRE · observed capital and income progress | Sum selected account balances in the latest month recorded by any selected account. Capital progress = capital ÷ goal, remaining = max(goal − capital, 0). Sum available credits and average only complete months, then divide the unrounded average by the income goal. | RUB, RUB/month, %; observation month and up to 12 completed Moscow months | Hide capital if a selected account is missing in the observation month. Flag income gaps, exclude incomplete months from average, include saved zeroes. Omit ratios without goals. | 60 + 40 against goal 200 gives 50% and 100 remaining. Two complete months with 20 and 0 credited give average 10. A third unreported month is a gap. | `/fire` |
| FIRE · projections | Use capital P, end-month flow C, monthly real rate r and n months. Real annual rate = (1 + nominal annual return) ÷ (1 + inflation) − 1. Convert to monthly via the twelfth root. Iterate balance = prior balance × (1 + r) + C. Solve for contribution to target, or find first month meeting goal. Recalculate per scenario and read value at target month. | Real RUB, RUB/month, months and target date | Require assumptions. Report unreachable goals explicitly. Use the zero-growth limit for zero return. Reconcile workbook timing conventions privately. | P = 100 and C = 10 at zero growth gives 220 in 12 months, a required flow of 10 and time to goal of 12 months for target 220. | Proposed FIRE scenarios |
| Currency · original and converted balances | Sum amounts by currency, then multiply by valuation-date RUB per unit and sum converted values. | Original currency and RUB at valuation date | Preserve original subtotals and flag missing rates. Withhold a complete RUB total when a rate is missing. | 3 USD + 2 USD at 90 RUB/USD plus 100 RUB gives original totals 5 USD and 100 RUB, and 550 RUB converted. | Proposed currency valuation (#89) |
| Deposits · principal, balance and maturity | Sum open principals separately from closing balances and credited interest. Group principal due by maturity and record funding separately. | RUB per month-end or maturity date | Keep unknown principal, balance and maturity visible as gaps. Report missing maturity separately from scheduled totals. | Open principals 100 and 200, balances 105 and 200, interest 5 and new funding 200 give principal 300 and balance 305. If only the first matures in June, principal due in June is 100. | Current `/investments/{month}` for balances and credits. Proposed deposits for terms and flows. |
| Bonds · position and coupon schedule | Quantity × nominal × clean price % gives clean value. Add quantity × accrued interest per unit for dirty value. Sum quantity eligible on each payment date × coupon per unit. | Units and RUB at valuation or coupon date | Keep amounts unknown when a required price, accrued figure or schedule is missing. Reconcile accrued-interest day count and eligibility privately. | 2 units at nominal 1,000 and 95% give 1,900 clean. With 10 accrued each, dirty value is 1,920. Coupon of 30 per eligible unit gives 60 due on its scheduled date. | Proposed bonds |
| Equity transactions · ledger and identifier checks | Add buys and subtract sells through the date. Gross trade amount = units × execution price. Match identifier to exactly one instrument and flag duplicate trade IDs. | Units and RUB per trade/as of date | Flag unknown or ambiguous instruments and incomplete trades before aggregation. Reconcile duplicate key and sale costing privately. | Buy 2 at 100 on Jan 10, then sell 1 at 120 on Feb 10. Position changes from 2 to 1, with 120 gross proceeds. | Proposed equity ledger |
| Equity overview · cost and quotes | Group by instrument, use remaining acquisition cost ÷ open units for average entry. Value = units × latest eligible dated quote. Unrealized difference = value − remaining cost. | RUB, RUB/unit and units at valuation date | Keep cost visible when no quote exists and leave value and difference unknown. No average at zero units. Reconcile disposal cost and quote age rules privately. | Buy 2 at 100 and 1 at 130. Before fees and sales, cost is 330 and average entry 110. A valid quote of 120 gives value 360 and difference 30. | Proposed equity holdings |
| Equity analysis · illustrative threshold check | Compare dated quote ÷ average entry − 1 against a configured threshold using ≥. | % and pass/fail per instrument at valuation date | Return unknown for missing quote or missing/nonpositive cost. With cost 45 and a second fund worth 40 at cost 35, group value is 100 and cost 80. | Proposed funds |

Budget product differences include a manually supplied planning-income baseline, annual averages based on elapsed calendar months with zero-filled actuals, past plan snapshots and whole-ruble rounding. The March examples use the [demo fixture][fixture]. The annual-rate and explicit-zero examples use the published [calculator][plan-test] and [budget regression tests][budget-test]. Any finer workbook equivalence requires private comparison. The investment review totals manual account observations and reports credited income and budget contributions independently. Market valuation, instruments and portfolio projections are candidate work.

## One-off decisions

A fictional December decision to defer buying a particular fund at that date belongs in a dated decision record. Its inputs and chosen action are specific to the scenario. The commitment total and cap comparison above can be rerun with different inputs and dates. Reconcile the original decision sheet privately before assigning further rules to the reusable scenario screen.
