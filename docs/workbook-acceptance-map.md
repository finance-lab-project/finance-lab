## Proposed budget report changes

- **Full-year plan (#80).** On `/years/{year}`, project the plan for each category. Months with no applicable rate contribute 0 RUB to the projection. A category with no applicable rate in any month has a projected total of 0 RUB. With only November and December recorded at 30,000 RUB/month and no explicit inception, project 30,000 back to January for 360,000 RUB/year. A 30,000 rate in January–June and 35,000 in July–December gives 390,000. An explicit July inception at 35,000 gives 210,000. Projection leaves actuals and carry unchanged.
- **Recorded-month average (#81).** On `/years/{year}`, for a November 2026 report, use November 2025 through October 2026. Four recorded values of 30,000, 0, 50,000 and 40,000 RUB give 30,000 RUB/month with coverage 4/12. Exclude unrecorded months from the denominator and show an empty state for zero coverage. This applies to income, spending, contributions and categories.
